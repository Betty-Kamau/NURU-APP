Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Nuru collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Who is responsible

Nuru decides how and why your personal data is used, and is responsible for it. You can reach us at bettykamau333@gmail.com or kelvinmuiga723@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

• Account details: your name, email, and phone number if you add one.

• Your profile, posts, comments and the groups you join.

• Study data: your progress, and notes, prayers and thanksgiving entries you choose to sync.

• Activity: event registrations, listings, and messages you send through Nuru.

• Payment records. We never store full card numbers or PINs.

• Basic device information, such as device type, app version and language.

• Your location, only if you allow it.

3. Location

We use your location to sort events and shop items by distance and to show them on the map. Listing and shop locations are rounded to an approximate area. You can turn location off at any time in Settings → Privacy.

4. How we use your information

• To run Nuru and give you the features you ask for.

• To keep Nuru safe, including automated and human checks of posts, listings and reports.

• To process payments and send receipts.

• To send the notifications you choose.

• To understand how Nuru is used and to improve it.

• To follow the law.

5. Why we may use it

Where the law asks for a reason, we rely on: our agreement with you (to provide the app), your consent (for example, location), our legitimate interest in keeping Nuru safe and improving it, and legal obligations.

6. Who we share it with

• Payment providers, to complete payments.

• Event organisers, when you register for their event (your name, contact details and answers).

• Service providers who host our servers or help us run Nuru, under contract and only for our purposes.

• Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

7. Your notes and journal

Your private notes, prayers and thanksgiving entries stay private. They are never shown to other people unless you post them.

8. Where your data is processed

Our service providers may process data in other countries. When they do, we take the steps the law requires to protect it.

9. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account we remove your personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law, such as payment receipts.

10. Your rights and choices

In Settings → Privacy you can make your account private, choose who can message you, turn off personalised promotions and location, download your data, or delete your account.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to some uses, and to withdraw your consent. To use these rights, email bettykamau333@gmail.com or kelvinmuiga723@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Kenya, the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner).

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. No service is completely secure, so please keep your sign-in details safe.

12. Children

We ask for your date of birth at sign-up to work out your age. If you are under 13, Community and the Shop are switched off for your account and we keep only the fact that you are under 13, not your date of birth.

If you are a parent or guardian and think your child has shared more with us than this, contact bettykamau333@gmail.com or kelvinmuiga723@gmail.com and we will delete the account.

13. Cookies and local storage

Nuru stores settings and progress on your device so the app works offline. See Cookies & Storage for details.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.

15. Contact us

Privacy questions or requests: bettykamau333@gmail.com or kelvinmuiga723@gmail.com. You can also call +254 712 350 117.
